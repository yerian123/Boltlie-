## Who we sell to

Independent **residential** HVAC company in Canada, outside Quebec. BC Lower Mainland
first, then Alberta and Ontario, then the US by email later.

- 3–15 technicians, roughly $1M–$5M revenue, owner-run, owner answers or is easy to reach
- Can fund about $1,500/month in ad spend
- Growing: hiring, room for more installs

**Highest-priority signals:** already paying for Google Ads or Local Services Ads;
shows "Closed" on weekday evenings; hiring a tech, installer, CSR or dispatcher now;
50–600 Google reviews at 4.0+; owner reachable on the main line.

**Not our customer:** franchises (Reliance, Enercare, Service Experts, One Hour,
Aire Serv), private-equity-owned groups, brands with 1,000+ Google reviews, suppliers,
commercial-only shops, shops with under ~20 reviews.

**Hard disqualifier: a shop that is booked solid.** Five owners in the quote bank say
some version of "I'm turning away half my work." They genuinely do not want what we
sell. If a thread author says this, do not treat them as a prospect.

**The wedge (HYPOTHESIS):** "busy" and "install schedule full" are different things.
October to March a shop can be buried in service calls while install slots sit open, and
every service call on a 20-year-old furnace is an unsold replacement. We sell converting
demand they already have, not lead volume.

## What we sell

Paid ads plus lead response and follow-up for residential HVAC shops.

**Order of emphasis, and this matters:** lead with install volume and converting existing
demand. The response and follow-up layer stays in the offer but never leads. Doc 02:
*"keep the text-back layer, stop leading with it, and never tell an owner he's losing
money on missed calls."*

## Who the operator is, in a thread

Someone who has scraped and called a few hundred independent residential HVAC shops
across BC and Ontario, and who builds automated lead response systems. Not a tech, not
an installer, never has been. This standing is real and it is interesting to that
audience. Do not inflate it.

## Industry data — PATTERN only, never quote as fact to an owner

- About 34% of calls answered live (ServiceTitan-linked, US)
- Callers who reach no one go elsewhere: 78% abandon, 82% call a competitor (CallRail 2025)
- Calling back within 5 minutes books 2–3x more jobs than within 60
- 50–70% of replacement estimates go unsold; 8–12 touches to close most
- Heat waves bring 3–5x calls, cold snaps 2–4x, office team stays the same size

## Evidence against us — read before writing anything

This is the part that keeps the agent honest.

- **Owners defend slow response rather than treating it as a leak.** A thread asking our
  exact question drew 9 owner replies; roughly 8 pushed back, 1 agreed. PATTERN, though
  the sample is 1–3 person US shops, not our ICP.
- **Fast callback reads as a bad signal in this trade:** *"I would worry about a small
  operator who promptly returns your calls… he just may not be that good, so he's not
  that busy."*
- **Hiring beats leads** in open-ended "what keeps you up at night" threads. Finding and
  keeping help won by a landslide.
- **Referrals and word of mouth, not ads**, are how these shops say they grow.
- **Our real competitor is a human receptionist.** Top reply in the 82-comment
  r/smallbusiness missed-call thread: *"You basically need to find a way to hire someone.
  It's losing you far more money than it would cost to hire a receptionist."*
- **The limit of our own offer, in an owner's words:** *"A text back buys time. Somebody
  still has to call with a real quote inside the hour for it to turn into a job."*

## Owner language worth using

- "I'm turning away half of my leads because I cannot handle the work load"
- "Those that couldn't wait will always call the next one in line" (PATTERN, 5 owners)
- "You don't need more jobs, you need more customers, 2 entirely different things"
- "tire kickers" (Canadian contractor, on paid directory leads)
- Angi/HomeAdvisor leads are "shared with 20+ contractors"

Objections to expect: *"Be super weary about marketing companies."* One owner burned
"$40,000 in three months" with an agency. *"Facebook will get you the cheapest of the
cheapest clients."*

## Never claim

- Any pricing, margin or install figure. Doc 03's $1,200–$3,600 gross profit is an
  ASSUMPTION and must not be stated anywhere.
- Rebates as a blanket promise. Canada Greener Homes closed Feb 2024; the interest-free
  loan stopped taking applications Oct 2025.
- Results, guarantees, or client outcomes of any kind.
- Any industry PATTERN figure as though it were measured fact.

## Seasons

October–March furnace and heat pump. April–September AC. Shoulder season (Apr–May,
Sep–Oct) and January are when owners say it goes quiet, which is when install slots sit
open. Never discuss furnace demand in July.

## Open gaps — do not paper over these

- Zero BC or Lower Mainland owners in the quote bank. One Canadian, a flooring contractor.
- Zero 3–15 tech shops talking about marketing. The biggest gap.
- No install prices or margins from any owner.
- Whether r/ProHVACR's 9,600 members are 3–15 tech shops or one-man-bands is unmeasured.
