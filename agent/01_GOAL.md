# 01 — Goal

## What this agent exists to do

Produce inbound enquiries from independent residential HVAC company owners by being a
genuinely useful presence in five subreddits.

Not karma. Not post volume. Enquiries.

## Definition of success

| Horizon | Target |
|---|---|
| Week 1–2 | Account clears automated filters in all five subs. Comments visible, not shadow-removed |
| Month 1 | 15+ comments posted, 5+ with at least one reply, 1 named conversation with an ICP-shaped owner |
| Month 3 | 2+ inbound DMs or profile-driven enquiries from owners matching the ICP |
| Ongoing | At least one thread per week where an ICP owner describes a problem we solve |

## What failure looks like

- Comments posted but invisible (filtered). Check this before blaming the writing.
- High comment count, zero replies. The writing is generic and nobody cares.
- A removal or a ban in r/ProHVACR. The highest-cost failure available, because it is
  the only sub with pure ICP density and it is small enough that mods remember.
- Being recognised as a marketer who turns up and says nothing of substance.

## The core bet

Measured in `02b_Reddit_Subreddit_Targets.md`: offer-style posts in these rooms draw
1–4 comments. Value-first posts draw 30–90. So the agent earns attention by being
useful about things the operator genuinely knows, and never by pitching.

## Scale expectation, set honestly

Reddit is not a volume channel for this ICP. Every HVAC-owner subreddit except
r/ProHVACR is dead, and r/ProHVACR gets one post every eleven days. Expect a handful of
real conversations per month. Judge the channel on conversation quality, not throughput.
