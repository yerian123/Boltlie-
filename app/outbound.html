Bring leads in from a spreadsheet

Option 1 · From Google Sheets (easiest)
  1. Open the sheet in Google Sheets.
  2. Copy the link from your browser's address bar.
  3. Paste it here and press Bring it in. The first time, Claude asks to use your Google Drive.
Option 2 · From a file on your computer

In Google Sheets or Excel: File → Download → "Comma-separated values (.csv)". That's just your spreadsheet saved as a simple file. Pick it here.

Columns are matched by name (Company, City, Phone, Owner, Rating, Reviews, Angle, Hours, Hook, Notes…). Shops already in your list are skipped.
